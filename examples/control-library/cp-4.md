# CP-4 — Contingency Plan Testing

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Methods for testing contingency plans to determine the effectiveness of the plans and identify potential weaknesses include checklists, walk-through and tabletop exercises, simulations (parallel or full interrupt), and comprehensive exercises. Organizations conduct testing based on the requirements in contingency plans and include a determination of the effects on organizational operations, assets, and individuals due to contingency operations. Organizations have flexibility and discretion in the breadth, depth, and timelines of corrective actions.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `cp-4_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `cp-4_smt.a`: Test the contingency plan for the system [PARAM cp-04_odp.01] using the following tests to determine the effectiveness of the plan and the readiness to execute the plan: [PARAM cp-4_prm_2].

- `cp-4_smt.b`: Review the contingency plan test results; and

- `cp-4_smt.c`: Initiate corrective actions, if needed.
