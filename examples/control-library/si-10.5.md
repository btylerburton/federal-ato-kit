# SI-10.5 — Restrict Inputs to Trusted Sources and Approved Formats

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Restricting the use of inputs to trusted sources and in trusted formats applies the concept of authorized or permitted software to information inputs. Specifying known trusted sources for information inputs and acceptable formats for such inputs can reduce the probability of malicious activity. The information inputs are those defined by the organization in the base control ( SI-10 (catalog reference: `#si-10`)).

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `si-10.5_smt`: Restrict the use of information inputs to [PARAM si-10.05_odp.01] and/or [PARAM si-10.05_odp.02].
