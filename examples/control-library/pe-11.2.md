# PE-11.2 — Alternate Power Supply — Self-contained

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The provision of a long-term, self-contained power supply can be satisfied by using one or more generators with sufficient capacity to meet the needs of the organization.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pe-11.2_smt`: Provide an alternate power supply for the system that is activated [PARAM pe-11.02_odp.01] and that is:

- `pe-11.2_smt.a`: Self-contained;

- `pe-11.2_smt.b`: Not reliant on external power generation; and

- `pe-11.2_smt.c`: Capable of maintaining [PARAM pe-11.02_odp.02] in the event of an extended loss of the primary power source.
