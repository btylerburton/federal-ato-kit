# AU-13 — Monitoring for Information Disclosure

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Unauthorized disclosure of information is a form of data leakage. Open-source information includes social networking sites and code-sharing platforms and repositories. Examples of organizational information include personally identifiable information retained by the organization or proprietary information generated by the organization.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `au-13_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `au-13_smt.a`: Monitor [PARAM au-13_odp.01] [PARAM au-13_odp.02] for evidence of unauthorized disclosure of organizational information; and

- `au-13_smt.b`: If an information disclosure is discovered:

- `au-13_smt.b.1`: Notify [PARAM au-13_odp.03] ; and

- `au-13_smt.b.2`: Take the following additional actions: [PARAM au-13_odp.04].
