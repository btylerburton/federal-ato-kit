# SA-15.7 — Automated Vulnerability Analysis

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Automated tools can be more effective at analyzing exploitable weaknesses or deficiencies in large and complex systems, prioritizing vulnerabilities by severity, and providing recommendations for risk mitigations.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sa-15.7_smt`: Require the developer of the system, system component, or system service [PARAM sa-15.07_odp.01] to:

- `sa-15.7_smt.a`: Perform an automated vulnerability analysis using [PARAM sa-15.07_odp.02];

- `sa-15.7_smt.b`: Determine the exploitation potential for discovered vulnerabilities;

- `sa-15.7_smt.c`: Determine potential risk mitigations for delivered vulnerabilities; and

- `sa-15.7_smt.d`: Deliver the outputs of the tools and results of the analysis to [PARAM sa-15.07_odp.03].
