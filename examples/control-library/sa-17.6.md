# SA-17.6 — Structure for Testing

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Applying the security design principles in SP 800-160-1 (catalog reference: `#e3cc0520-a366-4fc9-abc2-5272db7e3564`) promotes complete, consistent, and comprehensive testing and evaluation of systems, system components, and services. The thoroughness of such testing contributes to the evidence produced to generate an effective assurance case or argument as to the trustworthiness of the system, system component, or service.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sa-17.6_smt`: Require the developer of the system, system component, or system service to structure security-relevant hardware, software, and firmware to facilitate testing.
