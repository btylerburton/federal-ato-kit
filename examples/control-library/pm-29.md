# PM-29 — Risk Management Program Leadership Roles

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The senior accountable official for risk management leads the risk executive (function) in organization-wide risk management activities.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pm-29_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `pm-29_smt.a`: Appoint a Senior Accountable Official for Risk Management to align organizational information security and privacy management processes with strategic, operational, and budgetary planning processes; and

- `pm-29_smt.b`: Establish a Risk Executive (function) to view and analyze risk from an organization-wide perspective and ensure management of risk is consistent across the organization.
