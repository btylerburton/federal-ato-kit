# SA-4.11 — System of Records

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

When, by contract, an organization provides for the operation of a system of records to accomplish an organizational mission or function, the organization, consistent with its authority, causes the requirements of the PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) to be applied to the system of records.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sa-4.11_smt`: Include [PARAM sa-04.11_odp] in the acquisition contract for the operation of a system of records on behalf of an organization to accomplish an organizational mission or function.
