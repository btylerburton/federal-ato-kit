# AC-19.4 — Restrictions for Classified Information

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

None.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `ac-19.4_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `ac-19.4_smt.a`: Prohibit the use of unclassified mobile devices in facilities containing systems processing, storing, or transmitting classified information unless specifically permitted by the authorizing official; and

- `ac-19.4_smt.b`: Enforce the following restrictions on individuals permitted by the authorizing official to use unclassified mobile devices in facilities containing systems processing, storing, or transmitting classified information:

- `ac-19.4_smt.b.1`: Connection of unclassified mobile devices to classified systems is prohibited;

- `ac-19.4_smt.b.2`: Connection of unclassified mobile devices to unclassified systems requires approval from the authorizing official;

- `ac-19.4_smt.b.3`: Use of internal or external modems or wireless interfaces within the unclassified mobile devices is prohibited; and

- `ac-19.4_smt.b.4`: Unclassified mobile devices and the information stored on those devices are subject to random reviews and inspections by [PARAM ac-19.04_odp.01] , and if classified information is found, the incident handling policy is followed.

- `ac-19.4_smt.c`: Restrict the connection of classified mobile devices to classified systems in accordance with [PARAM ac-19.04_odp.02].
