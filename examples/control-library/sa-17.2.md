# SA-17.2 — Security-relevant Components

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The security-relevant hardware, software, and firmware represent the portion of the system, component, or service that is trusted to perform correctly to maintain required security properties.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sa-17.2_smt`: Require the developer of the system, system component, or system service to:

- `sa-17.2_smt.a`: Define security-relevant hardware, software, and firmware; and

- `sa-17.2_smt.b`: Provide a rationale that the definition for security-relevant hardware, software, and firmware is complete.
