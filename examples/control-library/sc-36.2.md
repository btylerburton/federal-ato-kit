# SC-36.2 — Synchronization

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

SC-36 (catalog reference: `#sc-36`) and CP-9(6) (catalog reference: `#cp-9.6`) require the duplication of systems or system components in distributed locations. The synchronization of duplicated and redundant services and data helps to ensure that information contained in the distributed locations can be used in the mission or business functions of organizations, as needed.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sc-36.2_smt`: Synchronize the following duplicate systems or system components: [PARAM sc-36.02_odp].
