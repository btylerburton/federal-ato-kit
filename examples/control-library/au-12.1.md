# AU-12.1 — System-wide and Time-correlated Audit Trail

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Audit trails are time-correlated if the time stamps in the individual audit records can be reliably related to the time stamps in other audit records to achieve a time ordering of the records within organizational tolerances.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `au-12.1_smt`: Compile audit records from [PARAM au-12.01_odp.01] into a system-wide (logical or physical) audit trail that is time-correlated to within [PARAM au-12.01_odp.02].
