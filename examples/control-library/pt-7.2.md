# PT-7.2 — First Amendment Information

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) limits agencies’ ability to process information that describes how individuals exercise rights guaranteed by the First Amendment. Organizations consult with the senior agency official for privacy and legal counsel regarding these requirements.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pt-7.2_smt`: Prohibit the processing of information describing how any individual exercises rights guaranteed by the First Amendment unless expressly authorized by statute or by the individual or unless pertinent to and within the scope of an authorized law enforcement activity.
