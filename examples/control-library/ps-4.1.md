# PS-4.1 — Post-employment Requirements

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Organizations consult with the Office of the General Counsel regarding matters of post-employment requirements on terminated individuals.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `ps-4.1_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `ps-4.1_smt.a`: Notify terminated individuals of applicable, legally binding post-employment requirements for the protection of organizational information; and

- `ps-4.1_smt.b`: Require terminated individuals to sign an acknowledgment of post-employment requirements as part of the organizational termination process.
