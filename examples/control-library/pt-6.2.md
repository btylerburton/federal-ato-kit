# PT-6.2 — Exemption Rules

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) includes two sets of provisions that allow federal agencies to claim exemptions from certain requirements in the statute. In certain circumstances, these provisions allow agencies to promulgate regulations to exempt a system of records from select provisions of the PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) . At a minimum, organizations’ PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) exemption regulations include the specific name(s) of any system(s) of records that will be exempt, the specific provisions of the PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) from which the system(s) of records is to be exempted, the reasons for the exemption, and an explanation for why the exemption is both necessary and appropriate.
