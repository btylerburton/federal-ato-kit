# IR-8.1 — Breaches

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Organizations may be required by law, regulation, or policy to follow specific procedures relating to breaches, including notice to individuals, affected organizations, and oversight bodies; standards of harm; and mitigation or other specific requirements.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `ir-8.1_smt`: Include the following in the Incident Response Plan for breaches involving personally identifiable information:

- `ir-8.1_smt.a`: A process to determine if notice to individuals or other organizations, including oversight organizations, is needed;

- `ir-8.1_smt.b`: An assessment process to determine the extent of the harm, embarrassment, inconvenience, or unfairness to affected individuals and any mechanisms to mitigate such harms; and

- `ir-8.1_smt.c`: Identification of applicable privacy requirements.
