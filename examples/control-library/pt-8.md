# PT-8 — Computer Matching Requirements

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) establishes requirements for federal and non-federal agencies if they engage in a matching program. In general, a matching program is a computerized comparison of records from two or more automated PRIVACT (catalog reference: `#18e71fec-c6fd-475a-925a-5d8495cf8455`) systems of records or an automated system of records and automated records maintained by a non-federal agency (or agent thereof). A matching program either pertains to federal benefit programs or federal personnel or payroll records. A federal benefit match is performed to determine or verify eligibility for payments under federal benefit programs or to recoup payments or delinquent debts under federal benefit programs. A matching program involves not just the matching activity itself but also the investigative follow-up and ultimate action, if any.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pt-8_smt`: When a system or organization processes information for the purpose of conducting a matching program:

- `pt-8_smt.a`: Obtain approval from the Data Integrity Board to conduct the matching program;

- `pt-8_smt.b`: Develop and enter into a computer matching agreement;

- `pt-8_smt.c`: Publish a matching notice in the Federal Register;

- `pt-8_smt.d`: Independently verify the information produced by the matching program before taking adverse action against an individual, if required; and

- `pt-8_smt.e`: Provide individuals with notice and an opportunity to contest the findings before taking adverse action against an individual.
