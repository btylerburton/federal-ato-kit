# PE-10 — Emergency Shutoff

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Emergency power shutoff primarily applies to organizational facilities that contain concentrations of system resources, including data centers, mainframe computer rooms, server rooms, and areas with computer-controlled machinery.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pe-10_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `pe-10_smt.a`: Provide the capability of shutting off power to [PARAM pe-10_odp.01] in emergency situations;

- `pe-10_smt.b`: Place emergency shutoff switches or devices in [PARAM pe-10_odp.02] to facilitate access for authorized personnel; and

- `pe-10_smt.c`: Protect emergency power shutoff capability from unauthorized activation.
