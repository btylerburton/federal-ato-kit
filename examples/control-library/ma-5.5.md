# MA-5.5 — Non-system Maintenance

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Personnel who perform maintenance activities in other capacities not directly related to the system include physical plant personnel and custodial personnel.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `ma-5.5_smt`: Ensure that non-escorted personnel performing maintenance activities not directly associated with the system but in the physical proximity of the system, have required access authorizations.
