# AT-4 — Training Records

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Documentation for specialized training may be maintained by individual supervisors at the discretion of the organization. The National Archives and Records Administration provides guidance on records retention for federal agencies.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `at-4_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `at-4_smt.a`: Document and monitor information security and privacy training activities, including security and privacy awareness training and specific role-based security and privacy training; and

- `at-4_smt.b`: Retain individual training records for [PARAM at-04_odp].
