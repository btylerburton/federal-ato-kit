# PS-6 — Access Agreements

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Access agreements include nondisclosure agreements, acceptable use agreements, rules of behavior, and conflict-of-interest agreements. Signed access agreements include an acknowledgement that individuals have read, understand, and agree to abide by the constraints associated with organizational systems to which access is authorized. Organizations can use electronic signatures to acknowledge access agreements unless specifically prohibited by organizational policy.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `ps-6_smt`: Statement grouping; see child parts.

- `ps-6_smt.a`: Develop and document access agreements for organizational systems;

- `ps-6_smt.b`: Review and update the access agreements [PARAM ps-06_odp.01] ; and

- `ps-6_smt.c`: Verify that individuals requiring access to organizational information and systems:

- `ps-6_smt.c.1`: Sign appropriate access agreements prior to being granted access; and

- `ps-6_smt.c.2`: Re-sign access agreements to maintain access to organizational systems when access agreements have been updated or [PARAM ps-06_odp.02].
