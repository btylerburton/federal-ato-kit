# PM-30.1 — Suppliers of Critical or Mission-essential Items

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

The identification and prioritization of suppliers of critical or mission-essential technologies, products, and services is paramount to the mission/business success of organizations. The assessment of suppliers is conducted using supplier reviews (see SR-6 (catalog reference: `#sr-6`) ) and supply chain risk assessment processes (see RA-3(1) (catalog reference: `#ra-3.1`) ). An analysis of supply chain risk can help an organization identify systems or components for which additional supply chain risk mitigations are required.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `pm-30.1_smt`: Identify, prioritize, and assess suppliers of critical or mission-essential technologies, products, and services.
