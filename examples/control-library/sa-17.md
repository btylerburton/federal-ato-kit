# SA-17 — Developer Security and Privacy Architecture and Design

## Catalog-grounded response guidance

Template guidance only. This material does not establish implementation, applicability, inheritance, or assessment status.

### Control-specific description (catalog guidance)

Developer security and privacy architecture and design are directed at external developers, although they could also be applied to internal (in-house) development. In contrast, PL-8 (catalog reference: `#pl-8`) is directed at internal developers to ensure that organizations develop a security and privacy architecture that is integrated with the enterprise architecture. The distinction between SA-17 and PL-8 (catalog reference: `#pl-8`) is especially important when organizations outsource the development of systems, system components, or system services and when there is a requirement to demonstrate consistency with the enterprise architecture and security and privacy architecture of the organization. ISO 15408-2 (catalog reference: `#87087451-2af5-43d4-88c1-d66ad850f614`), ISO 15408-3 (catalog reference: `#4452efc0-e79e-47b8-aa30-b54f3ef61c2f`) , and SP 800-160-1 (catalog reference: `#e3cc0520-a366-4fc9-abc2-5272db7e3564`) provide information on security architecture and design, including formal policy models, security-relevant components, formal and informal correspondence, conceptually simple design, and structuring for least privilege and testing.

### Requirements and exact statement parts

- `sa-17_smt`: Require the developer of the system, system component, or system service to produce a design specification and security and privacy architecture that:

- `sa-17_smt.a`: Is consistent with the organization’s security and privacy architecture that is an integral part the organization’s enterprise architecture;

- `sa-17_smt.b`: Accurately and completely describes the required security and privacy functionality, and the allocation of controls among physical and logical components; and

- `sa-17_smt.c`: Expresses how individual security and privacy functions, mechanisms, and services work together to provide required security and privacy capabilities and a unified approach to protection.
